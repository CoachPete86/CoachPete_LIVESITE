NeuroFit Coach Certification Program

Scale Your Impact Through Certified Coaches

Training fitness professionals worldwide to deliver Pete Ryan's proven NeuroFit methodology

Program Overview

The NeuroFit Coach Certification Program transforms qualified fitness professionals into certified NeuroFit coaches, enabling geographic expansion without direct overhead while maintaining program quality and brand integrity.

Revenue Projections: 300 certified coaches in Year 1 generating £360K+ in certification revenue plus ongoing renewal fees and commission-based income from coach-delivered programs.

Certification Level Structure

LEVEL 1 NeuroFit Foundations Coach

£799 certification fee | £149/year renewal

Prerequisites:

  • Current fitness certification (ACSM, NASM, ACE, or equivalent)
  • Minimum 1 year coaching experience
  • First aid/CPR certification

Curriculum (40 hours):

  • NeuroFit Methodology (8 hours): History, philosophy, core principles
  • Exercise Technique Mastery (12 hours): Proper form, progressions, regressions
  • Program Design Basics (8 hours): 12-week periodization, adaptation principles
  • Client Assessment (6 hours): Movement screening, goal setting
  • Mindset Coaching (6 hours): Habit formation, motivation techniques

Certification Process:

  • Online video modules with quizzes (32 hours)
  • Live virtual workshops (8 hours)
  • Written examination (2 hours, 80% pass rate)
  • Practical video submission evaluation

Authorized to Deliver:

  • Individual NeuroFit programs (Foundations & Core modules)
  • Small group classes (up to 12 participants)
  • Basic corporate wellness workshops

LEVEL 2 NeuroFit Performance Specialist

£1,299 certification fee | £199/year renewal

Prerequisites:

  • NeuroFit Level 1 Certification
  • 6 months experience delivering NeuroFit programs
  • 25+ client success stories documented

Advanced Curriculum (60 hours):

  • Neuroscience Deep Dive (12 hours): Brain-body connection, neuroplasticity
  • Advanced Periodization (10 hours): Sport-specific training, peaking protocols
  • Special Populations (12 hours): Seniors, athletes, injury rehabilitation
  • Business Development (8 hours): Marketing, client retention, pricing strategies
  • Advanced Assessment (10 hours): Cognitive testing, HRV analysis
  • Mentorship Training (8 hours): Coaching other trainers

Certification Process:

  • Advanced online modules (48 hours)
  • In-person intensive weekend (16 hours)
  • Case study presentation
  • Live coaching evaluation with master trainer

Authorized to Deliver:

  • All NeuroFit specialized modules (Women's, Combat Sports, etc.)
  • Corporate wellness consulting (up to £50K contracts)
  • Coach development workshops
  • Regional training seminars

LEVEL 3 NeuroFit Master Trainer

£1,999 certification fee | £299/year renewal

Prerequisites:

  • NeuroFit Level 2 Certification
  • 18 months NeuroFit coaching experience
  • Trained minimum 10 Level 1 candidates
  • Bachelor's degree in related field (preferred)

Master Level Curriculum (80 hours):

  • Research & Development (16 hours): Program innovation, outcome studies
  • Master Coach Skills (16 hours): Advanced behavior change, psychology
  • Instructor Development (16 hours): Teaching methodology, curriculum design
  • Business Mastery (16 hours): Franchise development, team building
  • Technology Integration (8 hours): App development, virtual coaching
  • Leadership & Mentoring (8 hours): Regional management, quality assurance

Master Certification Process:

  • Comprehensive online curriculum
  • 5-day intensive at NeuroFit headquarters
  • Original research project presentation
  • Teaching demonstration to Level 1 candidates
  • Annual recertification requirement

Authorized Privileges:

  • Certify Level 1 and Level 2 coaches
  • Develop regional training centers
  • Customize programs for enterprise clients
  • Represent NeuroFit at international conferences
  • Contribute to program development and research
