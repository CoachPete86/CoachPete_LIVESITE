Core Principles of the NeuroFit Philosophy
The Four Pillars of NeuroFit:

1. INTEGRATION: Brain and body work as one system
• Traditional training isolates muscle groups
• NeuroFit integrates movement patterns with cognitive challenges
• Every exercise engages multiple systems simultaneously

2. ADAPTATION: Progressive challenges create neuroplasticity
• The brain adapts to demands placed upon it
• Varying stimuli prevents plateaus and maintains engagement
• Challenges are individualised based on current capacity

3. RECOVERY: Growth happens during rest, not just training
• Active recovery is built into every session
• Stress and recovery cycles are carefully managed
• Mental recovery is as important as physical recovery

4. SUSTAINABILITY: Long-term adherence over short-term intensity
• Programs are designed for decades, not weeks
• Enjoyment and variety maintain motivation
• Progressive challenges prevent burnout

Lesson 2.1: Foundation Movement Patterns (180 minutes)

The Seven Fundamental Movements
1. SQUAT PATTERN

Primary Exercise: NeuroFit Squat
• Setup: Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out
• Movement: Hips back first, knees track over toes
• Depth: Thighs parallel or as mobility allows
• Breathing: Inhale down, exhale up

Cognitive Integration:
• Count backwards from 20 by 3s during the set
• Name a fruit/vegetable for each rep
• Balance a light object on the head

Common Errors:
• Knees caving inward (valgus collapse)
• Forward lean with rounded back
• Rising onto toes
• Not achieving adequate depth

Coaching Cues:
• "Sit back into an invisible chair"
• "Push the floor apart with your feet"
• "Chest proud, knees wide"
• "Drive through your heels to stand"

Progressions:
• Beginner: Chair-assisted squats
• Intermediate: Goblet squats with cognitive task
• Advanced: Single-leg squats with dual-task challenge

Regressions:
• Partial range of motion
• Hands holding stable surface
• Heel elevation for ankle mobility limitations
2. HINGE PATTERN

Primary Exercise: Romanian Deadlift
• Setup: Hip-width stance, shoulders back
• Movement: Hinge at hips, knees slightly bent
• Range: Until hamstring stretch, not back rounding
• Return: Drive hips forward to stand

Cognitive Integration:
• Spell name backwards during eccentric phase
• Recite days of week in different languages
• Balance reaction training with partner distraction

Module 3: Program Design Basics

Module Details

Duration: 8 hours | Format: Interactive workshops + case studies

Lesson 3.1: 12-Week Periodisation Framework (120 minutes)

The NeuroFit Periodisation Model:

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-3)
• Primary Focus: Movement quality and habit formation
• Training Frequency: 3-4 sessions per week
• Session Duration: 30-45 minutes
• Intensity: 60-70% perceived effort
• Cognitive Load: Simple, single-task challenges

Sample Week 2 Session:
• Warm-up: 5 minutes dynamic movement + breathing
• Movement Prep: 10 minutes foundational patterns
• Main Work: 20 minutes circuit (3 rounds, 45s work/15s rest)
• Cool-down: 10 minutes mobility + relaxation

Phase 2: Development (Weeks 4-6)
• Primary Focus: Strength building and skill acquisition
• Training Frequency: 4-5 sessions per week
• Session Duration: 45-60 minutes
• Intensity: 70-80% perceived effort
• Cognitive Load: Dual-task challenges introduced

Phase 3: Integration (Weeks 7-9)
• Primary Focus: Complex movement patterns and endurance
• Training Frequency: 5-6 sessions per week
• Session Duration: 45-75 minutes
• Intensity: 70-85% perceived effort
• Cognitive Load: Multi-task and environmental challenges

Phase 4: Performance (Weeks 10-12)
• Primary Focus: Peak performance and assessment
• Training Frequency: 5-6 sessions per week
• Session Duration: 60-90 minutes
• Intensity: 75-90% perceived effort
• Cognitive Load: Complex, unpredictable challenges

Lesson 3.2: Session Structure and Flow (90 minutes)

The Perfect NeuroFit Session Structure:

1. Neurological Activation (5-10 minutes)
Purpose: Prime the nervous system for learning and performance
Components:
• Breathing exercises (2 minutes)
• Light cardio movement (3 minutes)
• Dynamic mobility (3-5 minutes)
• Mental preparation/intention setting (1-2 minutes)

2. Movement Preparation (10-15 minutes)
Purpose: Rehearse movement patterns and activate stabilising systems
Components:
• Foundation pattern practice (5-8 minutes)
• Activation exercises (3-5 minutes)
• Balance and proprioception work (2-4 minutes)

3. Main Training Block (20-45 minutes)
Purpose: Primary training stimulus and skill development
Structure options:
• Circuit training (3-5 exercises, 2-4 rounds)
• Strength supersets with cognitive integration
• Skill practice with progressive challenges
• Interval training with recovery protocols

4. Integration Challenge (5-10 minutes)
Purpose: Apply skills under fatigue and complex conditions
Examples:
• Multi-directional movement with problem-solving
• Reactive balance challenges
• Partner-based unpredictable tasks
• Environmental adaptation exercises

5. Recovery and Reflection (10-15 minutes)
Purpose: Promote recovery and consolidate learning
Components:
• Active cool-down (5-7 minutes)
• Flexibility and mobility work (5-7 minutes)
• Breathing and relaxation (3-5 minutes)
• Session reflection and goal setting (2-3 minutes)

Session 1: Effective Coaching Communication (2 hours)

The NeuroFit Coaching Model: INSPIRE

I - INQUIRE: Ask powerful questions to understand client needs
• "What would success look like for you?"
• "When do you feel most energised during the day?"
• "What's worked well for you in the past?"

N - NORMALISE: Validate client experiences and challenges
• "That's completely normal - many clients report similar experiences"
• "You're not alone in facing these challenges"
• "It makes sense that you'd feel this way given your lifestyle"

S - SIMPLIFY: Break complex concepts into actionable steps
• Use analogies and metaphors
• Focus on one change at a time
• Provide clear, specific instructions

P - PRACTICE: Ensure mastery through repetition
• Demonstrate first, then guide practice
• Provide immediate feedback
• Allow time for skill consolidation

I - INTEGRATE: Connect new skills to daily life
• Discuss practical applications
• Identify potential obstacles
• Create implementation strategies

R - REINFORCE: Celebrate progress and maintain motivation
• Acknowledge improvements, however small
• Connect achievements to client goals
• Build confidence for continued progress

E - EVOLVE: Continuously adapt approach based on results
• Monitor progress regularly
• Adjust methods as needed
• Maintain long-term perspective
