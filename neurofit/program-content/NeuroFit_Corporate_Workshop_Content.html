Programme Overview

This comprehensive corporate wellness programme provides all content, scripts, assessments, and materials needed to deliver the NeuroFit methodology in workplace settings. Each workshop is designed for maximum engagement and measurable outcomes.

Workshop 1: Neuroscience of Peak Performance

Workshop Details

Duration: 60 minutes | Capacity: 10-50 participants

Required Materials:
  • Projector/screen for presentations
  • Printed handouts (provided below)
  • Yoga mats or towels for floor exercises
  • Timer for activities
  • Feedback forms

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the brain-body connection and its impact on work performance
  • Learn 3 evidence-based techniques to enhance focus and energy
  • Experience practical exercises that can be done at work
  • Develop personal action plan for workplace wellness

Workshop Timeline

Opening: Welcome & Energy Check-In (5 minutes)
Facilitator Script:
"Good morning, everyone. I'm [Name] and I'm here to share something that could transform how you feel and perform at work. Before we start, let's do a quick energy check-in. On a scale of 1-10, with 10 being peak energy and focus, where would you rate yourself right now? Just call out your number... [Allow responses]

Great! Let's see if we can move those numbers up by the end of our time together. Today we're exploring the fascinating world of neuroscience and how your brain and body work together to create peak performance at work."
Facilitator Notes: Note the average energy level - you'll reference this at the end. Use enthusiastic, confident tone. If energy is low (5 or below), acknowledge it: "I can see we need this session!"
Core Content: The Neuroscience Advantage (20 minutes)
Section 1: Your Brain at Work (8 minutes)
Key Points to Cover:
"Your brain uses 20% of your body's energy - more than any other organ. When you're stressed, tired, or inactive, your cognitive performance drops by up to 40%. Here's what happens...

[SHOW SLIDE: Brain under stress vs. optimised brain]

When you're stressed or sedentary:
• Cortisol blocks creative thinking
• Reduced blood flow limits problem-solving
• Decision fatigue sets in faster
• Memory consolidation suffers

When you're physically active and mentally calm:
• BDNF (brain fertiliser) increases by 200%
• Executive function improves
• Creative insights increase
• Stress resilience builds

The research is clear: Physical movement literally grows your brain and enhances cognitive performance."
Section 2: The Performance Stack (7 minutes)
Introduce the NeuroFit Performance Stack:
"Think of peak performance like a stack - each level supports the next:

Level 4: PEAK PERFORMANCE - Flow states, creative breakthroughs, leadership presence
Level 3: MENTAL CLARITY - Focus, decision-making, emotional regulation
Level 2: PHYSICAL ENERGY - Movement, strength, cardiovascular health
Level 1: FOUNDATION - Sleep, nutrition, stress management

Most workplace wellness programmes focus only on Level 1. We're going to work on all four levels to create sustainable peak performance."
Section 3: The Workplace Challenge (5 minutes)
Address Common Pain Points:
"Let me guess some challenges you face:
• 2pm energy crash
• Mind racing but can't focus
• Back and neck pain from desk work
• Feeling mentally foggy in meetings
• Sunday night anxiety about the week ahead

These aren't character flaws - they're predictable responses to how we've designed our work environment. The good news? Small changes create massive improvements."
Interactive Experience: The 5-Minute Reset (15 minutes)
Setup:
"Right now, we're going to experience the power of the NeuroFit method. This is our '5-Minute Reset' - a sequence you can do anywhere, anytime you need to boost energy and focus. I want you to notice how you feel before and after."

Pre-Assessment:
"First, rate your current energy and focus on that 1-10 scale again. Remember your number."

Movement Sequence (4 minutes):
"Everyone stand up. We're going to activate four key systems:

1. CIRCULATION (60 seconds):
• Arm circles - 10 forward, 10 backward
• Shoulder rolls - 10 each direction
• Gentle neck rotations - 5 each way
• Ankle rolls - 10 each foot

2. ACTIVATION (90 seconds):
• Standing marches - 30 seconds
• Desk push-ups - 10 reps (hands on desk/wall)
• Chair squats - 10 reps
• Standing side bends - 10 each side

3. BALANCE (60 seconds):
• Single leg stands - 15 seconds each leg
• Standing hip circles - 10 each direction
• Balance challenge - close eyes, 15 seconds

4. BREATHING (30 seconds):
• Box breathing - 4 counts in, hold 4, out 4, hold 4
• Repeat 4 times

Perfect! Now rate your energy and focus again. What changed?"
Facilitator Notes: Demonstrate each movement first. Encourage modifications for any physical limitations. Keep the energy high and positive. Most people will report 1-3 point increases in energy/focus.
Practical Application: Workplace Integration (15 minutes)
The Daily Performance Habits (10 minutes)
Morning Activation Protocol (5 minutes before work):
"Start your day by activating your nervous system:
• 2 minutes dynamic movement (we just did this)
• 1 minute intention setting
• 2 minutes deep breathing

Hourly Reset Reminders:
Set a phone alert every 90 minutes:
• Stand and stretch for 60 seconds
• Take 10 deep breaths
• Look at something 20+ feet away for 20 seconds

Afternoon Energy Protocol (2pm slump solution):
Instead of reaching for caffeine:
• 5-minute walk (stairs if possible)
• Hydrate with cold water
• Do our 5-minute reset sequence

End-of-Day Transition:
Before leaving work:
• Review 3 wins from the day
• Set 3 priorities for tomorrow
• Do 2 minutes of calming breathwork"
Personal Action Planning (5 minutes)
Individual Reflection:
"Take 3 minutes to complete your action plan worksheet:
1. Which energy dip time will you target first?
2. What's one movement you'll commit to doing hourly?
3. How will you remind yourself to do this?
4. Who will be your accountability partner?

Turn to someone nearby and share your plan. Accountability increases follow-through by 65%."
Closing & Next Steps (5 minutes)
Key Takeaways Summary:
"Today you've learned:
• Your brain and body are connected - movement enhances mental performance
• Small, consistent actions create significant results
• You have the tools to reset your energy anytime, anywhere

Your Challenge:
For the next 7 days, commit to the 5-minute morning activation and hourly movement breaks. Notice the difference in your energy, focus, and mood.

Resources:
You'll receive an email with:
• Video demonstrations of all exercises
• Printable desk exercise cards
• Progress tracking sheet
• Links to additional resources

Remember: Peak performance isn't about perfection - it's about consistency. Start small, stay consistent, and watch your work life transform."

Workshop 2: Stress Resilience & Mental Clarity

Workshop Details

Duration: 90 minutes | Capacity: 15-30 participants

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the neuroscience of stress and its impact on performance
  • Master 4 evidence-based stress management techniques
  • Learn to identify personal stress triggers and early warning signs
  • Create a personalised stress resilience plan
Opening: Stress Assessment & Goal Setting (10 minutes)
Stress Self-Assessment:
"Let's start with an honest assessment. I'll read some statements - give yourself a score from 1-5:

• I feel overwhelmed by my workload most days (1=never, 5=always)
• I have trouble switching off after work
• I feel physically tense (shoulders, jaw, back) during the day
• I struggle to concentrate on one task at a time
• I feel irritable or impatient more often than I'd like
• I have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep

Add up your scores. This is your stress baseline - we'll revisit this at the end.

Now, what's one area where you'd most like to see improvement? Turn to someone near you and share."
Core Content: The Stress-Performance Connection (25 minutes)
The Stress Response System (10 minutes)
Understanding Your Stress Response:
"Your stress response system evolved to keep you alive, but it's not designed for modern workplace pressures. Here's what happens:

Acute Stress (helpful):
• Increases focus and energy
• Enhances performance
• Boosts immune function
• Duration: Minutes to hours

Chronic Stress (harmful):
• Impairs memory and decision-making
• Reduces creativity and problem-solving
• Weakens immune system
• Increases risk of burnout
• Duration: Days, weeks, months

The goal isn't to eliminate stress - it's to build resilience and recovery."
The Resilience Framework (15 minutes)
The CALM Method:
"I'm going to teach you a framework that elite athletes and Navy SEALs use to manage stress:

C - CENTRE: Ground yourself in the present moment
A - ACKNOWLEDGE: Notice stress without judgment
L - LENGTHEN: Extend your exhale to activate recovery
M - MOVE: Use movement to metabolise stress hormones

This isn't just theory - we're going to practice each component."
Practical Training: CALM Technique Mastery (35 minutes)
CENTRE - Grounding Exercise (8 minutes)
5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique:
"This works anywhere, anytime. Let's practice:

Identify:
• 5 things you can see
• 4 things you can touch
• 3 things you can hear
• 2 things you can smell
• 1 thing you can taste

This immediately brings you into the present moment and stops stress spirals.

Workplace Application:
Use this before difficult meetings, challenging conversations, or when feeling overwhelmed."
ACKNOWLEDGE - Stress Awareness Training (7 minutes)
Body Scan for Stress Signals:
"Close your eyes and scan your body from head to toe. Notice:
• Tension in face, jaw, shoulders
• Breathing pattern - shallow or deep?
• Heart rate - calm or elevated?
• Stomach - tight or relaxed?

Now, instead of fighting these sensations, simply acknowledge them:
'I notice tension in my shoulders'
'I notice my breathing is shallow'
'I notice my mind is racing'

Acknowledgment without judgment is the first step to change."
LENGTHEN - Breath Control Training (10 minutes)
4-7-8 Breathing Technique:
"This activates your parasympathetic nervous system - your body's natural calm response:

• Inhale through nose for 4 counts
• Hold breath for 7 counts
• Exhale through mouth for 8 counts
• Repeat 4 times

Let's practice together... [Lead the exercise]

Alternative for Meetings - Box Breathing:
• Inhale for 4
• Hold for 4
• Exhale for 4
• Hold for 4

This can be done silently during any meeting."
MOVE - Stress Release Movements (10 minutes)
Desk-Based Stress Release Sequence:
"Movement metabolises stress hormones. Here's a 2-minute sequence you can do at your desk:

Upper Body Release (60 seconds):
• Shoulder rolls - 10 each direction
• Neck side bends - 5 each side
• Seated spinal twist - 5 each side
• Arm across body stretch - 15 seconds each

Lower Body Activation (60 seconds):
• Seated marches - 30 seconds
• Ankle rotations - 10 each foot
• Seated figure-4 hip stretch - 15 seconds each side

Do this sequence whenever stress levels rise."
Application & Planning: Personal Stress Management Plan (15 minutes)
Stress Trigger Identification:
"Complete your worksheet:

1. My Top 3 Stress Triggers:
• Trigger 1: _____________
• Trigger 2: _____________
• Trigger 3: _____________

2. My Early Warning Signs:
• Physical: _____________
• Mental: ______________
• Emotional: ___________

3. My CALM Action Plan:
• When I notice stress, I will CENTRE by: _____________
• I will ACKNOWLEDGE by saying: _____________
• My go-to LENGTHEN technique will be: _____________
• My preferred MOVE response is: _____________

4. Implementation Strategy:
• I will practice this daily at: _____________
• My reminder system will be: _____________
• My accountability partner is: _____________"
Closing & Resources (5 minutes)
Key Takeaways:
"Today you've learned:
• Stress isn't the enemy - chronic stress is
• You have immediate tools to shift your state
• Small, consistent practices build resilience
• The CALM method works in any situation

Your 30-Day Challenge:
Practice the CALM method once daily for 30 days. Track your stress levels weekly and notice the changes.

Resources Being Sent:
• CALM method reference card
• Guided audio recordings
• Weekly check-in template
• Advanced stress management techniques"

Facilitator Training Notes

  • Energy Management: Always model high energy and enthusiasm. Your state influences the group.
  • Modifications: Have alternatives ready for participants with physical limitations.
  • Timing: Use a visible timer. Keep activities moving but don't rush explanations.
  • Engagement: Ask for volunteers, use names when possible, encourage participation.
  • Follow-up: Send promised resources within 24 hours of the workshop.
  • Measurement: Always collect before/after energy ratings to demonstrate immediate impact.
