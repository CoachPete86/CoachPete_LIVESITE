Week 1-3: Foundation for Busy Professionals

Morning Power Start Routine (15 minutes)

Best performed: 6:00-6:15 AM, before checking emails

The Executive Wake-Up Circuit

  • Dynamic Warm-up (3 minutes)
    • Arm circles - 30 seconds
    • Leg swings - 30 seconds each direction
    • Torso twists - 30 seconds
    • Neck rolls and shoulder shrugs - 30 seconds
  • Core Power Circuit (10 minutes)
    • Push-ups - 60 seconds (modify on knees if needed)
    • Prisoner squats - 60 seconds
    • Mountain climbers - 60 seconds
    • Plank hold - 60 seconds
    • Burpees - 60 seconds
    • Rest 30 seconds between exercises
  • Energy Activation (2 minutes)
    • Jumping jacks - 30 seconds
    • Deep breathing (4-7-8 technique) - 90 seconds
Pro Tip: Lay out your workout clothes the night before. The 5-minute setup time is often the biggest barrier for morning routines.

Week 4-6: Stress Management Integration

High-Intensity Stress Buster (20 minutes)

Best performed: After work, before dinner

The Decompression Circuit

Equipment needed: None (can be done in hotel room or home office)

  • Warm-up (3 minutes)
    • Light cardio movement - marching in place
    • Joint mobility - all major joints
  • HIIT Circuit (14 minutes)
    • Perform each exercise for 45 seconds, rest 15 seconds
    • Complete 2 full rounds with 2-minute rest between rounds
    • High knees
    • Push-ups (modify as needed)
    • Jump squats
    • Mountain climbers
    • Burpees
    • Plank hold
    • Shadow boxing
  • Cool-down (3 minutes)
    • Deep breathing exercises
    • Progressive muscle relaxation
Stress Science: This circuit is designed to metabolise stress hormones (cortisol and adrenaline) that accumulate during high-pressure work situations. The combination of movement and breathwork activates the parasympathetic nervous system, promoting recovery.

Stress Management and Recovery

Executive Recovery Protocols

The 3-Minute Reset Technique

Use between meetings or during high-stress periods

  1. Minute 1: Breathe - Box breathing (4-4-4-4 count)
  2. Minute 2: Move - Gentle stretching or walking
  3. Minute 3: Mindset - Positive affirmations or gratitude

Sleep Optimization for Executives

2 Hours Before Bed
  • No more caffeine
  • Dim lights by 50%
  • Begin wind-down routine
1 Hour Before Bed
  • No screens or blue light
  • Light stretching or reading
  • Journal or meditation
30 Minutes Before Bed
  • Cool room to 65-68°F
  • Blackout curtains
  • White noise or earplugs
Bedtime Routine
  • Progressive muscle relaxation
  • 4-7-8 breathing technique
  • Gratitude practice
Executive Sleep Hack: Use the "10-3-2-1-0" rule: 10 hours before bed - no more caffeine; 3 hours before - no more food or alcohol; 2 hours before - no more work; 1 hour before - no more screens; 0 - the number of times you hit snooze.
