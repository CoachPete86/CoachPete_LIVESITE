  • React Native choice is smart for cross-platform consistency

  • Privacy Transparency: Users need clear data usage policies
  • Performance Concerns: AI processing could drain battery and overheat phones
  • Development Risk: Timeline seems ambitious for the complexity described

Recommendations for Improvement

  • Expand free tier to 5 workouts/week and 2 brain games to improve trial experience
  • Add comprehensive privacy section explaining AI data usage and storage
  • Include battery optimization strategies and performance benchmarks
  • Provide fallback options when AI features fail or aren't available
  • Add development risk mitigation plan and MVP feature prioritization

"Innovative concept with solid technical foundation, but needs privacy clarity and better freemium balance."
