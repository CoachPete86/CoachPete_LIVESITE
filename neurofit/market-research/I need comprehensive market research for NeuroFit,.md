## 3. Scientific Validation

- **Exercise \& Cognitive Performance**:
    - Systematic review (2025): Physical exercise significantly enhances executive function, attention, working memory, and cognitive flexibility in adolescents.[^1_17]
    - Acute moderate-intensity exercise reliably boosts cognitive performance in adults (inverted-U arousal theory).[^1_18]
    - Individual differences exist for high-intensity exercise; moderate intensity is best supported for productivity and focus.[^1_18]
- **Workplace Wellness Effectiveness \& ROI**:
    - Meta-analyses and case studies show reductions in health care costs, absenteeism, and presenteeism, alongside enhanced productivity and staff satisfaction.[^1_5][^1_6][^1_7]
- **Neuroscience-Based Programme Evidence:**
    - HKS Living Labs and Center for BrainHealth (2024): Corporate use of cognitive training improves measurable brain health indices and focus, with positive cultural impact.[^1_9]
    - Cari Wellbeing (UK): Measurable ROI and reduced stress in public sector teams using neuroscience-based interventions.[^1_8]
- **Stress Reduction \& Productivity:**
    - Programmes using hypnotherapy, focused movement, and mindfulness are cited for preventing burnout, increasing focus, and improving retention.[^1_12][^1_10][^1_19][^1_9]
