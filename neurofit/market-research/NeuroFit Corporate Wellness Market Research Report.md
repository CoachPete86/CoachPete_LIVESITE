## 3. Scientific Validation and Research

### Exercise-Cognitive Performance Research

Recent peer-reviewed studies support brain-body integration approaches:[^1_17][^1_18][^1_19]

**Key Research Findings:**

- **Optimal exercise dose: 500-1,499 MET minutes per week** shows strongest cognitive benefits[^1_17]
- **Outdoor exercise demonstrates superior cognitive enhancement** compared to indoor training[^1_19]
- **Multisensory workplace environments** significantly improve employee performance and perception[^1_18]

**Neuroscience Applications:**

- **Bayesian brain theory** supports multisensory integration in workplace design[^1_18]
- **Neuroplasticity research** validates targeted cognitive training effectiveness[^1_20]
- **Brain-body connection studies** show measurable improvements in executive function[^1_19]
