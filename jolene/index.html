Today's Learning Bite (5 minutes)

Why Protein at Breakfast Stops Your Body Eating Itself

Morning cortisol + no protein = muscle breakdown. 25g protein = repair mode. Today's meal gives exactly what you need.

The Science: Cortisol peaks when you're stressed - your body breaks down muscle for fuel. Regular eating times with quality protein help regulate stress hormones and preserve your strength.

Your Action: Focus on protein-rich breakfasts as your daily muscle insurance policy.

Week 1 Deep Dive: How Food Stress Works

  • Cortisol peaks when you're stressed - body breaks down muscle for fuel
  • Processed foods trigger more stress responses than whole foods
  • Regular eating times help regulate stress hormones

Why This Matters: Understanding this connection helps you make choices that support your energy, not drain it.
