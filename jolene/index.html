Walking Tips & Safety

Safety First: Always carry water, wear appropriate footwear, check weather conditions, and let someone know your route.
  • Start each walk with a 5-minute warm-up at a gentle pace
  • Maintain steady breathing - you should be able to hold a conversation
  • Cool down with 5 minutes of slower walking and gentle stretching
  • Listen to your body - rest if you feel pain or excessive fatigue
  • Build distance gradually - don't rush the progression
