SECTION 1: THE FOUNDATION

1.1 A Message for You, Norma

Alright, Mama Llama, the crusade starts now. You're officially back in the land of the living and in the Coach Pete Ryan family book. The physio sign-off was the green light; this plan is us hitting the accelerator. I have no doubt it's going to be fun. I expect I'll be sworn at, maybe even threatened, but I'm holding out for bribes (snacks and sweets always work). Time to get to work.

1.2 Our Mission: The Three 'Re's

Our mission for the next 15 weeks is simple and brutal. We're not 'training', we're rebuilding from the ground up.
1. Re-wire: We're going to force your body to forget the hesitation. Every rep will teach your ankle and wrist that they are strong, stable, and capable of handling the work.
2. Re-build: We will systematically hunt down any lingering weakness and replace it with solid, functional strength. Old points of injury become new armour.
3. Re-claim: This is the big one. We're extinguishing the 'what if' and replacing it with the confidence to move without fear. By the end of this, you won't just be back; you'll be better.

1.3 Your Roadmap: How to Use This Plan

Your plan is structured in two-week blocks, alternating between a **Strength Week** and a **Hybrid Week**. The circuits use a **45:15** work-to-rest ratio. You can click on any bolded exercise name to see instructions in a pop-up exercise card. Most importantly, listen to your body. Discomfort is where growth happens; sharp pain is a signal to stop and communicate with me.

SECTION 2: THE STRATEGY

2.1 Mission Critical: The "Chaturanga-Ready" Wrist Protocol

This is your dedicated homework, to be performed 3-4 times per week, even on rest days. The goal is to build load-bearing tolerance and control, paving the way for your chaturanga goal. Use your 2kg weight. Perform slowly.

  • Wrist Curls (Palm Up): 3 sets of 15 reps.
  • Reverse Wrist Curls (Palm Down): 3 sets of 15 reps.
  • Static Splint Press: Gently lean against a wall for 3 sets of 20-second holds.

2.2 Mission Critical: The "Trust Your Ankle" Protocol

This is your non-negotiable warm-up before every single session. We are actively retraining the neural pathways to eliminate hesitation and build confidence from the ground up.

  • Single-Leg Balance (Left Leg): 3 sets of 30-second holds.
  • Calf Raises: 2 sets of 20 reps, focusing on a slow, 3-second lowering phase.

SECTION 4: BEYOND THE WORKOUT

4.1 Fuelling Your Rebuild: Simple Nutrition Principles

You can't out-train a bad diet. To get the most out of these sessions, focus on these three non-negotiable principles:

  • Protein at Every Meal: Protein provides the building blocks to repair muscle tissue. Aim for a palm-sized portion with breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
  • Carbs Are Fuel: Don't fear carbs. They are your primary energy source. Focus on complex carbs like oats, brown rice, and sweet potatoes to power your workouts.
  • Hydration is Key: Water is essential for performance and recovery. Aim for 2-3 litres per day, more on training days.
Ready to Go Deeper?

These principles are the foundation. When you're ready to optimise your energy, accelerate recovery, and dial in your nutrition with precision, let me know. We can build a fully personalised nutrition plan that aligns perfectly with your training and goals.

4.2 Mindset: The Real Work

The workouts below are just instructions for your body. This section is the instruction manual for your mind. This is where the real transformation happens. Read this section before the first workout, and re-read it any time you feel doubt creeping in.

Winning the First Battle: The Five-Minute Rule

The hardest part of any workout is starting it. Motivation is a myth; discipline is a muscle. On days you feel you "can't be bothered," the only goal is to put your kit on and start the warm-up. That's it. Anyone can do five minutes. By the time those five minutes are up, the war against inertia is won, and you'll be ready to do the work.

Rewiring the Narrative: Every Rep is a Vote

Right now, your brain has a well-worn story about your ankle and wrist: "Be careful. It's weak. Don't trust it." This plan is designed to systematically dismantle that story. Every single pain-free rep of a lunge, every stable single-leg balance, every controlled wrist curl is a vote for a new narrative. It's tangible proof that your body is strong and adaptable. We are literally re-wiring your brain to associate these movements with strength, not fear. Focus on the successful reps, not the memory of the injury.

The Discomfort Dial: Learning the Difference Between Signals

Imagine you have a dial. On one end is **Discomfort**, on the other is **Pain**. Discomfort is the burning in your quads during a wall sit, the tremble in your core during a plank, the gasp for air after a finisher. This is the growth zone. We lean into this. Pain is a sharp, specific, "stop" signal from a joint. It's a red light. Your job is to become a master at reading this dial. We chase discomfort, but we respect and listen to pain. Always.

The Power of Visualisation: See it, Believe it, Do it

Before your physio review in a few weeks, I want you to start a new drill. Every night before you go to sleep, spend 60 seconds vividly imagining yourself performing a perfect, strong, controlled chaturanga. Feel the stability in your wrist, the strength in your shoulders, the control in your core. The brain struggles to tell the difference between a vividly imagined event and a real one. By doing this, you are preparing the neural pathways for success long before we even attempt the movement.

4.3 The Art of Recovery

You don't get stronger in the gym; you get stronger while you recover. Prioritise this.
Sleep: Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep per night. This is when your body repairs and rebuilds.
Active Recovery: Your Yoga Recovery days are a crucial part of the strategy. They help to reduce muscle soreness and improve mobility, allowing you to hit the next session harder.

SECTION 5: THE FINISH LINE & BEYOND

5.1 Measuring Success

Forget the scales. Our success metrics are different. After 15 weeks, we'll measure success by:
- Your confidence performing a single-leg balance on your left leg.
- The load tolerance and lack of pain in your wrist during the rehab drills.
- Your ability to complete 5 rounds of the final circuit without hesitation.
- The feeling of being strong, capable, and in control of your own body again.
